BEAM.bench

the merged engine, running. not the app. loading engine

1 · conformance -

The merged engine against the two shipped implementations, transcribed from laser-rig.html and detent-plot.html. This is the claim the whole repo rests on: one geometric model, two parameter sets. It is checked here in the browser against the same code your rigs run today.

2 · geometry -

Move the pointer over the field. The lattice is where whole axis units land, so its spacing is the real resolution at that spot. It is not uniform, and that is the non-linearity the planner has to respect.

3 · calibration

Four captured corners in axis units, ordered TL, TR, BR, BL, one per line as x,y. Paste what your board reports and see whether the solve is trustworthy before you plot through it. Residual under about 0.3 mm is a clean capture.

4 · wire

Build a packet from the segment list and see exactly what goes on the wire: escaped, CRC'd, and sized. Compare it against what your firmware expects. 


    

  


  

    
5 · planner guards

    

      

        The two guards a merged planner cannot ship without. An off-axis stroke is densified at the
        machine's own sampling step, quantised, and checked for zero length axis segments. One of
        those is a division by zero in the speed cap, and because Math.min(x, NaN) is
        NaN it poisons the whole velocity profile.
