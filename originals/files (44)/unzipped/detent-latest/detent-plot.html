quickstart

  1. Connect over USB or BLE. The app adopts whatever the board has saved, so a page reload costs you nothing.
  2. Leave limits OFF. Set invert so jog moves the beam the way you expect. Invert changes the motor, not the preview.
  3. Beam on, steer to each corner of your real target area, CAPTURE each one in order TL TR BR BL.
  4. SOLVE. Residual under about 0.3 mm means a clean capture. If the app warns about aspect, press FIT FIELD H.
  5. FROM CORNERS, then enforce limits. PERSIST writes everything to the board so it survives power cycles.

what the settings mean

throw is the distance from the Y mirror to the wall along the beam. mirror sep is between the two mirror pivots. field w and h are the real size of your target rectangle in mm, and content is laid out inside that rectangle, so if the field lies about its shape your drawing squashes or clips to match. draw and travel rates are half steps per second with beam on and off. lash comp is what the board inserts at each direction reversal; measured slack is what your gearbox really has, and the preview shows what any mismatch will look like on the wall. hold coils keeps the motors energized when idle, which holds position at the cost of warm motors.

the case aperture

Beam deflection is 0.1766 degrees per step, so at limits spanning N steps the cone is N x 0.1766 degrees wide. A window at distance d mm from the Y mirror must be at least 2 d tan(half angle) wide plus beam diameter. Cheaper than reprinting: capture your corners inside the clear opening and let FROM CORNERS make the limits your software aperture. The beam cone readout in LIMITS shows what the current values allow.

pushing speed

With the planner on, the draw and travel rates are ceilings for straights, not a constant. The planner decelerates into every corner and reversal and accelerates out, so raise the rates until STALL HUNT says stop, not until a square looks bad. Accel controls how hard it brakes: higher finishes faster but skips sooner. The hunt blinks the beam at home before each pass; mark that spot, and the first rate whose blink returns somewhere else is past your motor's pull-out. Set the draw rate to about seventy percent of the last clean rate.

drift

These motors are open loop, so a skipped step is forever. The firmware ramps out of every stop and every direction reversal, takes up backlash at the slow rate, and settles the coils before moving after an idle release. If it still walks, your rates are past what your units can pull: run the RAMP test pattern, listen for where it stalls, and set the draw rate to about sixty percent of that. Re zero with the ZERO button at a marked reference when needed.

beam safety

The board kills the beam five seconds after the host goes silent with nothing queued, and on any BLE disconnect. Shift drag fires the beam live; releasing shift before the pointer keeps it on until pointer up, which is deliberate, so watch your hand. STOP always flushes the queue and drops the beam.
